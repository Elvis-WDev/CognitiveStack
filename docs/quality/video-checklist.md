# Video Review Checklist

Use this checklist before a video ships. The first section is checked before production
starts. Review from the rendered file, at real size, first muted and then with sound.

## Before You Produce

- [ ] The video brief in `../video/index.md` is answered in writing: audience, problem, moment, cost, message, action, format.
- [ ] The video narrates one problem and lands one message.
- [ ] The storyboard exists, with one focal point per row and a named transition out of each scene.
- [ ] Three style frames passed review before animation started.

## Story

- [ ] The first frame is a finished composition, and the problem is visible within two seconds.
- [ ] Every beat escalates the problem; none repeats the previous one.
- [ ] The cost is concrete and countable.
- [ ] The turn is a deliberate stillness, and the resolution is visibly calmer and ordered.
- [ ] No person is mocked; the system is the source of the problem.
- [ ] Every number is sourced or explicitly framed as an example.
- [ ] A first-time viewer understands the story with the sound off.

## Visual Style

- [ ] One true isometric projection, fixed camera, no perspective or rotation.
- [ ] Every fill is a solid color: no gradient, glow, blur, grain, or texture.
- [ ] No outline or stroke on objects, characters, text, or icons.
- [ ] Every face is shaded from one light with the documented shading step.
- [ ] Every color comes from the token file, with the accent near 10 percent of the frame.
- [ ] The `problem` accent appears only in the problem phase, the `solution` accent only after the turn.
- [ ] Each moment has one focal point.

## Typography

- [ ] One family, two weights, embedded fonts.
- [ ] No text below 3.3 percent of the frame's short side.
- [ ] Every text meets its contrast ratio without outlines or shadows.
- [ ] Every text holds at least 1.0 s plus 0.3 s per word, and never less than 1.5 s.
- [ ] Copy is concrete, second person, present tense, and in the audience's locale.

## Motion

- [ ] Every narrative motion explains a change.
- [ ] Ambient micro-animations stay within their ranges, with at most three loops visible.
- [ ] Durations and easings come from `../video/motion.md`; no bounce or elastic easing.
- [ ] No crossfade, blur, glitch, spin, or camera shake.
- [ ] No dead time beyond the turn and the closing hold.
- [ ] Nothing flashes more than three times per second.

## Delivery

- [ ] Each format is recomposed, and text sits inside its safe areas and clear of platform interfaces.
- [ ] Frames decoded from the final files were reviewed, not only previews.
- [ ] Audio is licensed or original and mastered to about -14 LUFS and -1 dBTP.
- [ ] Captions exist for every spoken line.
- [ ] All deliverables in `../video/production.md` are present.
